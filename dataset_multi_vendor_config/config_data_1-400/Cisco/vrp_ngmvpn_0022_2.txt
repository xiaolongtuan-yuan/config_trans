bfd

 no shutdown

 no shutdown

 neighbor 4.4.4.4 update-source Loopback1
 !

  no synchronization
  neighbor 4.4.4.4 activate
  neighbor 4.4.4.4 send-community both
 !

  neighbor 4.4.4.4 activate
  neighbor 4.4.4.4 send-community both
 !
