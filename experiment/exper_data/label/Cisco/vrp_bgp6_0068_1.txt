 no shutdown

 no shutdown

  neighbor 3.3.3.3 activate
  no synchronization

  neighbor 3.3.3.3 activate
  no synchronization
  redistribute connected
  neighbor 3.3.3.3 label advertise
  neighbor 2001:db8:1::1 activate
 exit-address-family

  network 2.2.2.2 0.0.0.0
  network 10.0.0.0 0.0.0.3
