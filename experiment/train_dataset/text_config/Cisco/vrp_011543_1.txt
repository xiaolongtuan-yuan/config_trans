hostname CE2

vlan 2
!
cfm version standard
interface GigabitEthernet1/0/1
 switchport mode trunk
 switchport trunk allowed vlan 2
 no shutdown
!
ethernet cfm mode on
ethernet cfm md md3
 ethernet cfm ma ma3
  ethernet cfm ma vlan 2
   ethernet cfm mep 4 interface GigabitEthernet1/0/1 direction egress
   ethernet cfm mep 4 ccm-send enable
   ethernet cfm remote-mep 3
   ethernet cfm remote-mep 3 ccm-receive enable
   ethernet cfm delay-measure receive test-id 1 mep 4
   ethernet cfm delay-measure send test-id 2 mep 4 remote-mep 3 interval 1000
!
